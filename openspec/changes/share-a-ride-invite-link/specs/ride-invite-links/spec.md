## Purpose

What a ride invite token is, what holding one grants, what it never grants, when it dies, who may
mint and revoke one, and what every rider who can hold a URL sees — for every role that can reach
a token: signed-out visitor, un-onboarded rider, ordinary signed-in rider, existing crew member,
the ride's organizer, a rider blocked in either direction, and a rider who has already claimed it.

**Possession of the token is the credential.** That is the sentence this capability turns on, and
it is the only grant in this schema that is not a fact about an identity. Everything below exists
to bound it: what the string buys, how long it buys it for, who can stop it, and what stays true
after it dies.

**The token adds no audience arm to any policy.** `rides` SELECT and `private.can_read_ride` are
untouched by this change. A token buys two RPC calls; after a claim the rider's reach is `083`'s
live-invite arm, identical to an accepted in-app invitee. A requirement below asserts that
directly, because "it only grants the ride" is exactly the kind of summary that is wrong by one
surface.

**Every requirement is a statement about a role and a resource, so each maps onto an assertion in
`supabase/tests/rls_test.sql`.** Three are named as exceptions where they are stated: the
always-a-tap rule is a component contract, the stash's storage medium is a client contract, and
the landing screen's one-message-for-every-dead-token rule is a rendering contract.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The token SHALL be generated by the database and SHALL NOT be nameable by any client

`public.ride_invite_links.token` SHALL take its value from a column default of
`encode(extensions.gen_random_bytes(16), 'hex')` — **128 bits, 32 lowercase hexadecimal
characters** — and SHALL carry `unique` and `check (token ~ '^[0-9a-f]{32}$')`.

The INSERT grant on `public.ride_invite_links` SHALL name `(id, ride_id, created_by)` and SHALL
NOT name `token`, `expires_at`, `created_at` or `revoked_at`. **The grant is the guarantee and the
default is not**: a default applies only when the column is omitted, and PostgREST will happily
name one.

No client role SHALL hold UPDATE on the table, so a token cannot be rotated onto an existing row.

128 bits SHALL be treated as sufficient that guessing is not an attack, and no rate limit, ledger
or lockout SHALL be built for the claim path on the strength of guessing alone.

#### Scenario: A client cannot choose a token
- **WHEN** any rider inserts a `ride_invite_links` row naming `token`
- **THEN** it SHALL be refused with `42501`, from the column grant and not from a trigger

#### Scenario: A client cannot choose an expiry
- **WHEN** any rider inserts a row naming `expires_at`, `created_at` or `revoked_at`
- **THEN** it SHALL be refused with `42501`

#### Scenario: Two links never share a token
- **WHEN** many links are created
- **THEN** `token` SHALL be `unique`, so a claim resolves to at most one link and the resolution
  SHALL NOT depend on ordering

#### Scenario: No client role holds UPDATE
- **WHEN** `has_table_privilege` is asked for `authenticated` and for `anon` on
  `public.ride_invite_links` for UPDATE
- **THEN** both SHALL be false, asserted **per grantee** rather than by a grant-row count, since
  `postgres` and `service_role` hold everything by Supabase default
- **AND** no UPDATE policy SHALL exist on the table

### Requirement: Only the ride's organizer SHALL mint or revoke a link

`public.ride_invite_links` INSERT SHALL be permitted only where `created_by = auth.uid()` **and**
the caller is the ride's `organizer_id`, evaluated by an `EXISTS` against `public.rides` under the
caller's own row security.

`public.revoke_ride_invite_link(link uuid)` SHALL set `revoked_at` for exactly one link whose
ride the caller organizes, and SHALL have **one raise site**, so "no such link", "not your ride"
and "already revoked" are indistinguishable.

Widening minting to the crew SHALL be a separate change with its own scenarios. Handing N riders
the power to mint bearer tokens for a private ride is a different security statement from handing
one, and it is not the same widening as `083`'s invite policy.

#### Scenario: The organizer mints a link
- **WHEN** a ride's organizer inserts a row naming themselves as `created_by`
- **THEN** it SHALL succeed, with `token` and `expires_at` server-supplied and `revoked_at` NULL

#### Scenario: A crew member cannot mint
- **WHEN** a rider holding a `ride_members` row for the ride, who is not its organizer, inserts
- **THEN** it SHALL be refused with `42501` by the INSERT policy

#### Scenario: A club member cannot mint
- **WHEN** a member of the ride's club who is neither organizer nor crew inserts
- **THEN** it SHALL be refused

#### Scenario: A rider cannot mint on someone else's behalf
- **WHEN** any rider inserts a row whose `created_by` is not `auth.uid()`
- **THEN** it SHALL be refused, even where that `created_by` names the ride's actual organizer

#### Scenario: A non-organizer cannot revoke
- **WHEN** any rider who does not organize the link's ride calls `revoke_ride_invite_link`
- **THEN** it SHALL raise, with the same message and SQLSTATE as a link id that does not exist

#### Scenario: A signed-out visitor reaches nothing
- **WHEN** a request for `public.ride_invite_links` arrives with no session
- **THEN** zero rows SHALL be returned and every write SHALL be refused, because `anon` holds no
  grant on the table and this change SHALL add none, per decision #1

### Requirement: A link SHALL die at its ride's departure and no later than 14 days from creation

`expires_at` SHALL be set by a BEFORE INSERT trigger to
`least(rides.departure_at, now() + interval '14 days')` for the link's ride.

**Liveness SHALL re-read `rides.departure_at` at every use** and SHALL NOT rely on `expires_at`
alone, because a ride may be edited to depart earlier after its link was minted.

Liveness SHALL be defined **once**, in `private.live_ride_invite_link(t text)`, and both the
preview and the claim SHALL obtain it from there. A link is live when **all** of: the token
matches a row; `revoked_at` is NULL; `now() < expires_at`; the ride still exists; and
`now() < rides.departure_at`.

`expires_at` comparisons SHALL be made on instants. `rides.timezone` (`080`) is not consulted:
the wall-clock rule governs how a departure is *rendered*, never when it *occurs*.

#### Scenario: A link for a ride two days out expires with the ride
- **WHEN** a link is minted for a ride departing in two days
- **THEN** `expires_at` SHALL equal the ride's `departure_at`

#### Scenario: A link for a ride three months out is capped
- **WHEN** a link is minted for a ride departing in three months
- **THEN** `expires_at` SHALL be `created_at + 14 days`, not the departure

#### Scenario: Moving the ride earlier shortens the link
- **WHEN** a ride is edited to depart before its link's `expires_at`
- **THEN** the link SHALL stop being live at the new departure, without `expires_at` being rewritten

#### Scenario: The two callers cannot disagree
- **WHEN** the liveness predicate is changed
- **THEN** it SHALL be changed in `private.live_ride_invite_link` alone, and both
  `ride_invite_link_preview` and `claim_ride_invite_link` SHALL be asserted to answer identically
  for the same token in every one of the dead states below

### Requirement: A live token SHALL buy exactly two RPC calls and no policy reach

Holding a token SHALL permit calling `public.ride_invite_link_preview(t text)` and
`public.claim_ride_invite_link(t text)`, both granted to `authenticated` only.

It SHALL NOT grant, widen or bypass any row-level policy. **This change SHALL add no audience arm
to `public.rides`, and SHALL NOT modify `private.can_read_ride`.**

`public.ride_invite_link_preview` SHALL return **exactly** these named columns for exactly one
ride: ride id, title, `departure_at`, `timezone`, `meeting_point`, the organizer's username, the
organizer's avatar path, and a crew **count**. It SHALL NOT return the crew's ids or usernames,
the ride's chat, `ride_members` rows, any second ride, the club's roster, or any column of
`profiles` beyond the organizer's username and avatar.

The preview SHALL be a fixed column list in SQL and SHALL NOT return `rides.*`, so a column added
to `rides` later is not disclosed by default.

#### Scenario: The preview discloses no roster
- **WHEN** a token holder previews a ride with crew
- **THEN** a count SHALL be returned and no rider id or username of any crew member SHALL be

#### Scenario: A token reaches exactly one ride
- **WHEN** a token holder previews or claims
- **THEN** exactly the link's own `ride_id` SHALL be reachable, and no other ride SHALL become
  readable by any route

#### Scenario: The ride policy is untouched
- **WHEN** `pg_policy` is read for `public.rides` SELECT after this change applies
- **THEN** its qual SHALL be byte-identical to the string the suite pinned before it, and
  `private.can_read_ride`'s `prosrc` SHALL be unchanged

#### Scenario: A token grants nothing without a session
- **WHEN** either RPC is called with no session
- **THEN** it SHALL be refused, because `anon` holds no EXECUTE on either and this change SHALL
  grant none

#### Scenario: A token does not open the chat before a claim
- **WHEN** a token holder who has not claimed reads `ride_messages` for that ride
- **THEN** zero rows SHALL be returned, because `private.is_ride_crew` is untouched by this change

### Requirement: Every dead token SHALL be indistinguishable from every other, and from a guess

`public.ride_invite_link_preview` SHALL return **zero rows** for every non-live case, and SHALL
NOT raise. `public.claim_ride_invite_link` SHALL have **one raise site**, with one message and one
SQLSTATE, reached by every non-live case.

The landing screen SHALL render **one message** for all of them, and SHALL NOT tell the rider
which one occurred.

The one permitted exception is the participation gate, which raises from
`private.join_ride_from_invite` with `check_violation`. That is a fact about the caller
themselves and discloses nothing about the token — `083`'s own reasoning, restated because the
one-raise-site rule would otherwise appear to forbid it.

#### Scenario: An expired token
- **WHEN** a token whose `expires_at` has passed is previewed or claimed
- **THEN** the preview SHALL return zero rows and the claim SHALL raise the single error
- **AND** no `ride_invites` row and no `ride_members` row SHALL be written

#### Scenario: A revoked token
- **WHEN** a token whose `revoked_at` is not NULL is previewed or claimed
- **THEN** the outcome SHALL be identical to the expired case, by inspection of both the message
  and the SQLSTATE

#### Scenario: A token for a deleted ride
- **WHEN** the ride is deleted and its token is then presented
- **THEN** the link row SHALL already be gone by cascade, and the outcome SHALL be identical to a
  token that never existed

#### Scenario: A token for a ride that has already departed
- **WHEN** `now()` is past the ride's `departure_at` and the token is presented
- **THEN** the outcome SHALL be identical to the expired case, **including** where `expires_at` is
  still in the future because the ride was moved earlier

#### Scenario: A malformed or guessed token
- **WHEN** a string that is not 32 hexadecimal characters, or is well-formed but matches no row,
  is presented
- **THEN** the outcome SHALL be identical to every case above
- **AND** the claim SHALL NOT raise a different error for a malformed string than for an unmatched
  one, since a distinct parse error confirms the token format to a prober

#### Scenario: A blocked rider, block in either direction
- **WHEN** a rider who has blocked the organizer, or whom the organizer has blocked, presents a
  live token
- **THEN** the preview SHALL return zero rows and the claim SHALL raise the single error
- **AND** the block SHALL be checked **in the RPC's own body**, since a `security definer`
  function has no policy beneath it to carry decision #2
- **AND** no `ride_invites` row SHALL be written before the check, so no residue remains that a
  later unblock could activate

#### Scenario: Blocking is checked in both directions by one call
- **WHEN** the block check is written
- **THEN** it SHALL use `private.is_blocked`, which is symmetric, and SHALL NOT test a directional
  `blocks` row

### Requirement: A rider who has not completed onboarding SHALL NOT be admitted

The participation gate SHALL be carried by `private.join_ride_from_invite`, which already restates
it. `claim_ride_invite_link` SHALL NOT re-implement it and SHALL NOT bypass it.

A gate trigger on `ride_members` cannot fire for a `security definer` writer — every gate trigger
carries `when (current_user = 'authenticated')` and `current_user` inside a definer body is the
owner — so the restatement in `join_ride_from_invite` is the only thing standing between an
account created by calling GoTrue's `/auth/v1/signup` directly and a `ride_members` row. This is
`078`'s case exactly.

`public.ride_invite_links` SHALL itself carry an `enforce_participation_gate` trigger on INSERT,
because minting a link is participation.

#### Scenario: An un-onboarded rider cannot claim
- **WHEN** a rider whose `terms_accepted_at` or `onboarding_completed_at` is NULL calls
  `claim_ride_invite_link` with a live token
- **THEN** it SHALL raise with `check_violation` from `private.join_ride_from_invite`
- **AND** no `ride_members` row SHALL exist for them afterwards

#### Scenario: The gate restatement is still present
- **WHEN** `private.join_ride_from_invite`'s body is read
- **THEN** it SHALL still contain the `profiles` check on both stamps, asserted directly, since
  this change adds a caller to that function and a later edit removing the check would silently
  ungate two paths

#### Scenario: An un-onboarded rider cannot mint
- **WHEN** such a rider inserts a `ride_invite_links` row
- **THEN** the gate trigger SHALL refuse it with `23514`

### Requirement: A claim SHALL write one accepted invite and one crew row, in that order, and SHALL be idempotent

`claim_ride_invite_link` SHALL, for a live token and an unblocked, onboarded caller:

1. insert into `public.ride_invites` with `invitee_id = auth.uid()`, `inviter_id` = the link's
   `created_by`, `status = 'accepted'`, `responded_at = now()`, `link_id` = the link;
   `on conflict (ride_id, invitee_id) do update` setting `status = 'accepted'` and
   `responded_at = now()`, **preserving the existing `inviter_id`**;
2. then call `private.join_ride_from_invite(auth.uid(), ride)`.

**The order SHALL NOT be reversed.** `join_ride_from_invite` re-checks
`private.can_read_ride`, and on a private clubless ride the live-invite arm is the only arm that
can make it true — so calling it first returns `false`, writes nothing, and admits nobody, with no
error raised.

`private.join_ride_from_invite` SHALL NOT be modified by this change. It gains a second caller and
nothing else.

The RPC SHALL take the **token** and SHALL NOT take a ride id or a rider id. The subject is
`auth.uid()` and the resource is the link.

#### Scenario: A stranger claims a private clubless ride
- **WHEN** a signed-in, onboarded rider with no other route to the ride claims a live token
- **THEN** an `accepted` `ride_invites` row and a `going` `ride_members` row SHALL both exist
- **AND** the ride SHALL be readable to them afterwards through `083`'s existing live-invite arm

#### Scenario: The same rider claims twice
- **WHEN** the same rider claims the same token a second time
- **THEN** it SHALL succeed without error, no second `ride_invites` row SHALL exist by the
  `unique (ride_id, invitee_id)` key, no second `ride_members` row SHALL exist by
  `on conflict do nothing`, and **the use count SHALL NOT increase**

#### Scenario: A rider already crew claims
- **WHEN** a rider who already holds a `ride_members` row for the ride claims
- **THEN** their existing row SHALL be untouched, keeping its `status` and `joined_at` — a rider
  who answered `maybe` SHALL NOT be silently rewritten to `going`
- **AND** an `accepted` `ride_invites` row SHALL be written, so their read of the ride does not
  depend on which arm admitted them

#### Scenario: The organizer opens their own link
- **WHEN** the ride's organizer presents their own token
- **THEN** the preview SHALL render, because the organizer reads their own ride by the top-level
  arm
- **AND** the claim SHALL be refused by `ride_invites`' `check (invitee_id <> inviter_id)` with
  `23514`, since a link's `created_by` is the organizer
- **AND** the surface SHALL NOT offer them a Join control, reading their own organizer status
  rather than relying on the refusal

#### Scenario: A rider with a pending in-app invite claims
- **WHEN** a rider already holding a `pending` invite for the ride claims the link
- **THEN** the conflict branch SHALL take, `status` SHALL become `accepted`, `inviter_id` SHALL
  keep its original value, and `link_id` SHALL be set

#### Scenario: A rider with a declined in-app invite claims
- **WHEN** a rider holding a `declined` invite claims the link
- **THEN** it SHALL become `accepted` and they SHALL join, consistent with `083`'s rule that the
  invitee alone may reopen their own refusal — a link they were given is their own affirmative act,
  not the inviter re-sending

#### Scenario: A revoked link does not eject anyone
- **WHEN** a link is revoked after riders have claimed it
- **THEN** every `ride_invites` and `ride_members` row it produced SHALL be untouched
- **AND** deleting the link row SHALL set `link_id` to NULL and SHALL NOT delete those rows

### Requirement: The organizer SHALL see each link's use count, and the count SHALL be derived

The count SHALL be the number of `public.ride_invites` rows carrying that `link_id`, read under
the organizer's own row security. It SHALL NOT be a counter column incremented on claim.

The surface SHALL NOT present the number as an immutable ledger, because it is read through a
block-dominated policy: a rider who claimed and later blocks the organizer stops being visible to
them, and the count goes down. That is decision #2 working as designed.

There SHALL be **no maximum-uses ceiling**, on PD-293's reasoning: a cap nobody can see is worse
than no cap, and here the rider who hits it is a stranger whose only message would be
indistinguishable from expiry and revoke.

#### Scenario: Two riders claim, the organizer sees two
- **WHEN** two riders claim the same link
- **THEN** the organizer's read of that link SHALL report a use count of 2

#### Scenario: Repeat claims do not inflate it
- **WHEN** one of them claims again
- **THEN** the count SHALL stay 2

#### Scenario: A block reduces it
- **WHEN** one claimer blocks the organizer
- **THEN** that `ride_invites` row SHALL stop being readable to the organizer and the count SHALL
  report 1, with no error and no gap in the list

#### Scenario: There is no cap
- **WHEN** any number of riders claim a live link
- **THEN** none SHALL be refused for a reason relating to how many came before them

### Requirement: The landing route SHALL be public, SHALL render no data without a session, and SHALL define every state

`/rides/join` SHALL be added to `PUBLIC_PATHS` in `src/lib/auth/guard.ts`, which is a denylist of
public paths — so this is a deliberate opening and the only one this change makes.

**The token SHALL be a query parameter, not a path segment.** `next.config.ts` ships
`output: 'export'`; a dynamic segment requires `generateStaticParams`, which cannot enumerate
secrets. This is forced, not preferred.

With no session the route SHALL render the shell, a generic sentence naming neither the ride nor
its organizer, and controls to sign in or create an account. It SHALL NOT call either RPC, and
SHALL NOT render the ride's title, date, meeting point, organizer or crew count. Decision #1 is
unchanged and no `anon` grant is added to make this screen richer.

The route SHALL define all seven states from `client-render-shell`'s checklist:

| State | What it renders |
|---|---|
| No session | Shell, generic invite copy, Sign in / Create account. No ride data. |
| Loading | A skeleton of the preview card. Gated on the **data**, never on `isLoading`. |
| Live token, signed in | The preview and one `Join this ride` control. |
| Dead token (any of the six) | One message: the invite link is no longer valid. Plus a route to `/rides`. |
| Error (the RPC failed) | Distinguishable from a dead token, with a retry. A failed read is not a decided answer. |
| Offline | The cached preview if one exists, with the Join control **disabled** and stated as such — a claim is a write and SHALL NOT be queued. |
| Already claimed | The preview with the control replaced by a route to the ride. |

`undefined` SHALL mean "not yet" and `null` SHALL mean "decided"; only the second renders the dead
message.

#### Scenario: A visitor with no session sees no ride
- **WHEN** a signed-out visitor opens a live link
- **THEN** no ride title, date, meeting point, organizer name or crew count SHALL be rendered from
  any source, including a cache left by a previous session
- **AND** neither RPC SHALL be called

#### Scenario: A dead token and a failed read are told apart
- **WHEN** the preview RPC errors, as opposed to returning zero rows
- **THEN** the screen SHALL offer a retry and SHALL NOT tell the rider the link is invalid

#### Scenario: The guard sends an un-onboarded rider through the wizard first
- **WHEN** a rider with a session but no completion stamp reaches the landing route
- **THEN** the route guard SHALL send them to their resume step, per decision #5
- **AND** the token SHALL survive that journey and return them to the landing route afterwards

### Requirement: The token SHALL survive the auth round trip without being spendable by another rider

The token SHALL be held in `sessionStorage` under one key, and SHALL NOT be held in
`localStorage`, a cookie, or the query cache. A capability token SHALL NOT outlive its tab.

Sign-out SHALL clear it, per `client-session-storage`'s standing rule that sign-out destroys every
local trace of the rider.

**The URL is the durable copy.** A rider whose stash is lost — a different browser, a closed tab —
SHALL be able to re-open the same link and claim, and no recovery mechanism beyond that SHALL be
built.

**A claim SHALL be a user-initiated action, always.** No effect, no route-guard branch and no
session listener SHALL call `claim_ride_invite_link`. This is a component contract rather than a
policy, and it is the only thing preventing a stash left by an abandoned sign-up from being spent
by whichever rider signs in next on that browser — a wrong-rider claim that is a perfectly valid
claim at the database layer and therefore invisible to every assertion in `supabase/tests/`.

#### Scenario: Abandoned sign-up, different rider signs in
- **WHEN** rider A opens a link, abandons sign-up, and rider B signs in in the same tab
- **THEN** rider B SHALL NOT be joined to the ride by any automatic path
- **AND** the landing screen SHALL show rider B the preview and require an explicit tap

#### Scenario: The confirmation email opens in another browser
- **WHEN** a new rider signs up, confirms in a different browser, and has no stash there
- **THEN** they SHALL land signed in with nothing broken, and re-opening the WhatsApp link SHALL
  claim normally

#### Scenario: Sign-out clears the stash
- **WHEN** a rider signs out with a token stashed
- **THEN** the key SHALL be absent afterwards

#### Scenario: The token leaves the address bar
- **WHEN** the landing route has read the token
- **THEN** it SHALL be removed from the visible URL, so it does not sit on a shared screen or ride
  a `Referer`
- **AND** this SHALL be understood as reducing exposure, not eliminating it: the token still
  reaches the browser's history and the static host's access log, which is inherent to a
  capability URL and is bounded by expiry and revoke

### Requirement: The link SHALL open the web app rather than the native shell, and this SHALL be stated

Until universal links ship (PD-205), a rider with the app installed who taps the link gets a
browser. This is accepted, and SHALL be stated in the PR body rather than discovered.

Nothing SHALL be lost by it: the claim writes to the database, so the ride is on the rider's list
when they next open the shell.

`shareAppLink` SHALL be used unchanged. It already resolves `canonicalOrigin()` rather than
`window.location.origin`, which is what stops a link minted inside the native shell from pointing
at `https://localhost`.

#### Scenario: A link minted in the shell is followable
- **WHEN** an organizer inside the native build shares a link
- **THEN** the URL SHALL carry the canonical origin and SHALL NOT carry `https://localhost`

### Requirement: A link SHALL die with its ride and hold no personal data beyond its creator

`ride_invite_links.ride_id` SHALL cascade on ride delete and `created_by` SHALL cascade on profile
delete, so `029`'s erasure contract reaches the table from both ends.

`ride_invites.link_id` SHALL be `on delete set null`, so deleting a link removes the attribution
and never the rider.

The table SHALL hold no free text, no recipient list and no record of who a link was sent to — it
cannot, because decision 1 makes the link open. **Retention is therefore the ride's**: links live
as long as their ride and are removed with it, and no separate window is owed.

#### Scenario: Deleting the ride removes its links
- **WHEN** a ride is deleted
- **THEN** every `ride_invite_links` row for it SHALL be gone, and every token SHALL behave as
  never having existed

#### Scenario: Deleting the organizer's account removes their links
- **WHEN** a rider deletes their account
- **THEN** every link they minted SHALL be gone by cascade

#### Scenario: A deleted claimer leaves no orphan
- **WHEN** a rider who claimed a link deletes their account
- **THEN** their `ride_invites` row SHALL cascade away and the use count SHALL fall accordingly
