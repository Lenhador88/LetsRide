# ride-chat-unread (delta)

> **Added at archive (2026-09-21), and it removes the capability in full.** `ride-chat-unread` was
> standing only because `add-ride-chat-unread` archived ahead of this change, in the order `design.md`
> D11 recommends; this change's proposal already drops everything it specifies — `public.ride_reads`,
> `public.stamp_ride_read()` and `public.ride_has_unread(uuid)` go in `109`. Every requirement below
> is about the retired watermark table, so each is removed rather than modified.
>
> **Where the rule goes rather than dies:** the unread mark survives on the replacement, stated in
> `ride-threads` — *"Messages SHALL page by keyset cursor, and the unread mark SHALL extend the
> watermark model"* — over `ride_thread_reads`. A removal here is a statement about `ride_reads`,
> never a repeal of the per-rider watermark or of the refusal of read receipts.

## REMOVED Requirements

### Requirement: The unread mark SHALL be a per-rider, per-ride watermark and SHALL NOT be a per-message read table

**Reason:** `public.ride_reads` is dropped by `109`.

**Migration:** the per-rider watermark is restated for threads in `ride-threads` over `ride_thread_reads`.

### Requirement: A rider SHALL write a watermark only for themselves, and only for a chat they can actually read

**Reason:** as above — there is no chat and no `ride_reads`.

**Migration:** as above.

### Requirement: A watermark SHALL NOT be readable by anyone but its owner, and this app SHALL NOT have read receipts

**Reason:** as above.

**Migration:** as above; the refusal of read receipts is not repealed by this removal.

### Requirement: The unread answer SHALL be computed under the caller's own row security, and SHALL NOT be denormalised

**Reason:** `public.ride_has_unread(uuid)` is dropped by `109`.

**Migration:** as above.

### Requirement: A rider's own messages SHALL NOT light their own dot

**Reason:** as above.

**Migration:** as above.

### Requirement: A rider with no watermark SHALL have a defined starting point, including the organizer who holds no crew row

**Reason:** as above.

**Migration:** as above.

### Requirement: The watermark timestamp SHALL be generated by the database, never by the device

**Reason:** `public.stamp_ride_read()` is dropped by `109`.

**Migration:** as above.

### Requirement: The watermark SHALL advance while the rider is on the screen, not only when they arrive

**Reason:** the chat screen that advanced it is deleted.

**Migration:** as above.

### Requirement: The mark SHALL be a dot and SHALL NOT be a count

**Reason:** the chat's dot is retired with the chat.

**Migration:** as above.

### Requirement: The dot SHALL define every state it can be in, and SHALL NOT render a stale or speculative mark

**Reason:** as above.

**Migration:** as above.

### Requirement: Watermarks SHALL die with their ride and their rider, and their retention SHALL be stated

**Reason:** there are no `ride_reads` rows left to retain.

**Migration:** as above.

### Requirement: Every role's reach into a ride's unread state SHALL be stated

**Reason:** as above.

**Migration:** as above.

### Requirement: The surfaces this change does not build SHALL be named rather than half-built

**Reason:** as above.

**Migration:** as above.
